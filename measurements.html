MEASUREMENT NOTES

HOW TO MEASURE.

  1. 01

    Lay the garment flat

    Place the garment face up on a hard, flat surface. Smooth out folds without stretching the fabric. Fasten the placket and align the side seams.

  2. 02

    Measure edge to edge

    All widths on this page are flat measurements taken from edge to edge in a straight line, not around the body. Do not double the value.

  3. 03

    Keep the tape square

    Hold the tape parallel to the surface and perpendicular to the seam being measured. Record to one decimal place.

  4. 04

    Send a reference garment where possible

    A physical reference garment removes ambiguity. Note in the production notes if one is being couriered to the Bangalore facility.

Measurements marked PENDING are optional. If you are unsure of a value, leave it blank and add a note below — we will confirm it with you before production.
